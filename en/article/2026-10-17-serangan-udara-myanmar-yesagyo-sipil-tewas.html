Myanmar Military Airstrike in Yesagyo: 15 Civilians Killed,...

Stateline MEDIA Newsroom•October 17, 2026•2 min read

Myanmar's military carried out an airstrike on a Starlink internet shop in Thit Gyi Taw Village, Yesagyo Township, Magway Region, on Friday afternoon (10/16/2026). The death toll has reached 15 civilians, including children.

Table of Contents

  1. Afternoon Strike on an Internet Shop
  2. Toll Still Being Verified
  3. Background: Airstrikes on the Rise

Key Facts

  • Myanmar's military carried out an airstrike using jet fighters and helicopters on a Starlink internet shop in Thit Gyi Taw Village, Yesagyo Township, Magway Region, on Friday (10/16/2026) at around 3:00 p.m. local time.
  • The death toll has reached 15 civilians, including children — 14 from Thit Gyi Taw Village and 1 from Thit Kauk Seik Village — according to the independent outlet Khit Thit Media, cited by Global Arakan Network on Saturday (10/17/2026).
  • The victims were people using the internet at the shop as well as young children from houses near the site; the toll is said to potentially rise further, and the exact list is still being verified.
  • Local residents say the situation on the ground remains chaotic: military drones keep hovering over the village, panicked villagers are fleeing, and phone lines have been cut off.
  • The strike is part of the Myanmar junta's escalating aerial campaign against areas accused of supporting the armed resistance; UN investigators (IIMM) reported in August 2026 that Myanmar's military was increasingly attacking civilians with drones and motorized paragliders, with a significant escalation ahead of elections scheduled for December–January.
  • Myanmar's military has not commented on the attack.

Afternoon Strike on an Internet Shop

Myanmar's military carried out an airstrike on a Starlink internet shop in Thit Gyi Taw Village, Yesagyo Township, Magway Region, on Friday (10/16/2026) at around 3:00 p.m. local time, using jet fighters and helicopters.

The victims were people using the internet at the shop as well as young children from houses near the site. According to the independent outlet Khit Thit Media, cited by Global Arakan Network on Saturday (10/17/2026), the death toll has reached 15 civilians, including children — 14 from Thit Gyi Taw Village and 1 from Thit Kauk Seik Village.

Toll Still Being Verified

Local residents say the death toll could still rise, as the exact list of victims is being verified. The situation on the ground remains chaotic: military drones keep hovering over the village, panicked villagers are fleeing, and phone lines have been cut off.

Background: Airstrikes on the Rise

The strike is part of the Myanmar junta's escalating aerial campaign against areas accused of supporting the armed resistance. UN investigators (IIMM) reported in August 2026 that Myanmar's military was increasingly attacking civilians with drones, motorized paragliders, and low-cost aircraft, with a significant escalation ahead of elections scheduled for December–January.

Myanmar's military has not commented on the Yesagyo strike.
