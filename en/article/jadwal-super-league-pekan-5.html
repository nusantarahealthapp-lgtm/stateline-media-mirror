Super League Week 5 Fixtures: Persita vs Persija Headlines

Stateline MEDIA Newsroom•October 14, 2026•3 min read

The 2026/2027 BRI Super League (Indonesia's top flight) enters week five on October 16-18, 2026, with eight matches. Persita Tangerang vs Persija Jakarta on Saturday (17/10) at 19:00 WIB is the week's big match.

Key Facts

  • The 2026/2027 BRI Super League's fifth week runs October 16-18, 2026, with eight matches over three days.
  • Friday (16/10): PSIM Yogyakarta vs PSM Makassar (15:30 WIB), Java United FC vs Bali United FC (19:00), Madura United FC vs Bhayangkara Presisi Lampung FC (19:00), Persijap Jepara vs Isenmulang Kalteng FC (19:00).
  • Saturday (17/10): Garudayaksa FC vs Arema FC (15:30 WIB), Persita Tangerang vs Persija Jakarta (19:00 WIB) at Banten International Stadium.
  • Sunday (18/10): Persebaya Surabaya vs PSS Sleman (15:30 WIB), Persib Bandung vs Persik Kediri (19:00 WIB) at Si Jalak Harupat Stadium.
  • The week's big match pits league leaders Persija Jakarta against unbeaten Persita Tangerang (two wins, two draws).
  • Borneo FC and Dewa United sit out this week, having already played five matches; all games air live on Indosiar and stream on Vidio. (WIB = Western Indonesian Time.)

Super League Week 5: Eight Matches in Three Days

The 2026/2027 BRI Super League continues into week five on October 16-18, 2026. Eight matches are scheduled over three days, with several intriguing clashes on the slate.

The week opens with PSIM Yogyakarta vs PSM Makassar on Friday (16/10/2026) at 15:30 WIB at Sultan Agung Stadium. Three more games follow at 19:00 WIB: Java United FC vs Bali United FC, Madura United FC vs Bhayangkara Presisi Lampung FC, and Persijap Jepara vs Isenmulang Kalteng FC.

On Saturday (17/10/2026), Garudayaksa FC meet Arema FC in the afternoon before the headline act, Persita Tangerang vs Persija Jakarta, at 19:00 WIB at Banten International Stadium. The week closes on Sunday (18/10/2026) with Persebaya Surabaya vs PSS Sleman (15:30 WIB) and Persib Bandung vs Persik Kediri (19:00 WIB).

Persita vs Persija: A Consistency Test for the Kemayoran Tigers

Persita Tangerang vs Persija Jakarta is the standout fixture of week five. Persija arrive as league leaders after winning all four opening matches, including a 4-0 rout of PSIM Yogyakarta.

Persita are no easy opponents. The Cisadane Warriors are unbeaten in four matches with two wins and two draws. Head coach Carlos Pena says his side keep working to fix shortcomings ahead of facing the table-toppers.

In two home games, Persita had to fight until the final minutes for winning goals against Java United FC and Garudayaksa FC, while on the road they let leads slip in 1-1 and 2-2 draws with PSIM and PSM Makassar respectively.

PSIM vs PSM Open the Week

The opener between PSIM Yogyakarta and PSM Makassar brings together two sides beaten in week four: PSIM were thrashed 4-0 by Persija, while PSM lost 0-3 at home to PSS Sleman on October 11, 2026.

Both will target a bounce-back in front of their fans, making this a chance to end their negative runs.

Persib Bandung, hosting Persik Kediri on Sunday night, will also chase maximum points to stay near the top.

How to Watch: Live on Indosiar and Vidio

All week-five BRI Super League 2026/2027 fixtures are scheduled to air live on Indosiar and stream on Vidio. Mark your calendar so you don't miss your club in action.

Borneo FC and Dewa United sit out this week, having already played five matches. Fixtures are subject to change at the competition operator's discretion.
