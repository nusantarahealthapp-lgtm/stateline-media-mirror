Lazio Stun Juventus 3-0 in Turin as Danish Duo Shine

Stateline MEDIA Newsroom•October 19, 2026•3 min read

Lazio beat Juventus 3-0 at the Allianz Stadium in Serie A Matchday 7 in the early hours of Monday (19/10/2026) WIB. Goals from Oliver Provstgaard (37'), Gustav Isaksen (66') and Fisayo Dele-Bashiru (89') took the Biancocelesti top of the table alongside Inter; Juventus are stuck in eighth.

Key Facts

  • Lazio beat Juventus 3-0 in Serie A Matchday 7 at the Allianz Stadium in Turin on Sunday night (18/10/2026) local time; kickoff was 01:45 WIB with full time around 03:35 WIB on Monday (19/10/2026).
  • Lazio's goals came from Oliver Provstgaard with a header in the 37th minute (assist Mattia Zaccagni), Gustav Isaksen in the 66th (assist Nuno Tavares), and substitute Fisayo Dele-Bashiru in the 89th with a solo run from his own half.
  • The win was Lazio's first Serie A away victory in Turin since October 2017. Lazio now have 19 points and sit top of the table alongside Inter, while Juventus are stuck in eighth with 11 points.
  • Gennaro Gattuso became the first Lazio coach to win six of his first seven Serie A matches (one draw). Juventus played without coach Luciano Spalletti on the touchline as he served a one-match ban following his red card against Cagliari.
  • Juventus had a goal by Randal Kolo Muani in the 24th minute ruled out for offside. Home fans jeered the hosts' slow second-half play; Juve's best chances came from a Kolo Muani header over the bar and a Francisco Conceição curler comfortably saved by Christos Mandas.

Danish Duo Dismantle Juventus in Turin

Lazio produced a masterclass to demolish Juventus 3-0 in Serie A Matchday 7 at the Allianz Stadium in the early hours of Monday (19/10/2026) WIB. The opener came from Danish defender Oliver Provstgaard in the 37th minute: meeting Mattia Zaccagni's cross with a glancing header from six yards — his first goal for the capital club.

Gustav Isaksen doubled the lead in the 66th minute. The Danish winger controlled Nuno Tavares' cross-field pass, cut inside past Zeki Celik and curled a left-footed finish into the far bottom corner. The sealing goal came from substitute Fisayo Dele-Bashiru in the 89th minute with a stunning individual effort: running from inside his own half, beating Gleison Bremer, then firing past Guglielmo Vicario from a tight angle.

Frustrated Juventus, Spalletti in the Stands

Juventus played without coach Luciano Spalletti in the technical area as he served a touchline ban following his red card against Cagliari. Assistant Marco Domenichini, leading from the sidelines, could not spark a Bianconeri side that looked slow and predictable.

The hosts had a goal by Randal Kolo Muani in the 24th minute ruled out for offside. In the second half, home fans loudly jeered their team's aimless possession play. The best chances came from a Kolo Muani header that sailed over and a Francisco Conceição curler that proved too easy for goalkeeper Christos Mandas.

Lazio Top, Juventus Languish in Eighth

The victory took Lazio to the top of the Serie A table with 19 points, level with Inter — and their first league win in Turin since October 2017. Gennaro Gattuso set a record as the first Lazio coach to win six of his opening seven Serie A matches.

Juventus, by contrast, languish in eighth place with 11 points from seven games. Pressure is mounting on Spalletti, whose side looked devoid of ideas against Lazio's intensity. Next up, Juventus host Parma while Lazio travel to Lecce.
