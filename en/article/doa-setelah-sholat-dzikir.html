Dua After Prayer: Recommended Dhikr Recitations

Stateline MEDIA Newsroom•October 14, 2026•2 min read

After obligatory prayers, Muslims are encouraged to recite dhikr before supplicating. Here is the order: istighfar, tasbih, tahmid, takbir, tahlil, Ayat al-Kursi, and dua.

Key Facts

  • The order of dhikr after obligatory prayers: istighfar 3 times, “Allahumma antas salaam wa minkas salaam, tabaarakta yaa dzal jalaali wal ikraam”, then tasbih, tahmid, takbir 33 times each, and tahlil.
  • Tasbih “Subhanallah” 33 times, tahmid “Alhamdulillah” 33 times, takbir “Allahu Akbar” 33 times — this sequence is based on authentic hadith narrated by Bukhari and Muslim.
  • It is recommended to recite Ayat al-Kursi once after every obligatory prayer.
  • After dhikr, make supplication, for example “Rabbanaa aatinaa fid dunyaa hasanah wa fil aakhiroti hasanah wa qinaa ‘adzaaban naar” — asking for good in this world, the hereafter, and protection from Hell.
  • Dhikr after prayer acknowledges Allah's blessings with tongue and heart, and brings tranquility after fulfilling the obligation.

The Order of Dhikr After Obligatory Prayers

After the salam, Muslims are encouraged not to leave immediately but to recite dhikr first. Begin with istighfar — “Astaghfirullah” — three times, as an admission of sins and a plea for forgiveness.

Continue with the supplication: اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ، تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ — “Allahumma antas salaam wa minkas salaam, tabaarakta yaa dzal jalaali wal ikraam.” Meaning: “O Allah, You are Peace and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, O Possessor of majesty and honor.”

Tasbih, Tahmid, Takbir, and Tahlil

The main dhikr continues with tasbih “Subhanallah” (Glory be to Allah) 33 times, tahmid “Alhamdulillah” (all praise to Allah) 33 times, and takbir “Allahu Akbar” (Allah is Greatest) 33 times. The sequence closes with tahlil: “Laa ilaaha illallaah, wahdahu laa syariika lah, lahul mulku wa lahul hamdu wa huwa ‘alaa kulli syai’in qodiir.”

This practice is based on authentic hadith: the Prophet SAW taught his companions to recite tasbih, tahmid, and takbir 33 times each after every prayer, as a deed that lets them catch up with those ahead in goodness (narrated by Bukhari and Muslim).

Ayat al-Kursi and Closing Dua

It is also recommended to recite Ayat al-Kursi once after every obligatory prayer: “Allahu laa ilaaha illaa huwal hayyul qayyuum...” through “...wahuwal ‘aliyyul ‘azhiim” (Quran Al-Baqarah: 255). This verse carries many virtues and protections.

After the dhikr sequence, make personal supplications, for example: رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ — “Rabbanaa aatinaa fid dunyaa hasanah wa fil aakhiroti hasanah wa qinaa ‘adzaaban naar.” Meaning: “Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of Hell.”

The Virtue of Dhikr After Prayer

Not everyone is granted the health, time, and opportunity to pray — ending it with dhikr is a way of acknowledging that blessing with tongue and heart. Dhikr after prayer calms the soul, strengthens faith, and keeps one's spiritual bond with Allah SWT alive throughout the day.

Make post-prayer dhikr a habit. This light practice carries great reward and marks a servant's love for their Lord.
