AC Milan Edge Atalanta 3-2, Unbeaten Run Continues

Stateline MEDIA Newsroom•October 19, 2026•4 min read

AC Milan beat Atalanta 3-2 in Serie A matchday 7 at San Siro in the early hours of Monday (19/10/2026) WIB. Goals from Goncalo Ramos (25' pen), Adrien Rabiot (43'), and Mario Gila (57') put Milan 3-0 up before Gianluca Gaetano (66') and Nikola Krstovic (81') replied. Milan remain unbeaten in seven matches.

Key Facts

  • AC Milan beat Atalanta 3-2 in a Serie A matchday 7 clash at San Siro on Sunday (18/10/2026), kickoff 23:00 WIB (full-time around 00:51 WIB Monday 19/10). Milan led 2-0 at half-time; the second half finished 1-2.
  • Goncalo Ramos put Milan ahead from the penalty spot in the 25th minute — awarded after VAR confirmed a handball by substitute Relja Obric blocking Yunus Musah's shot.
  • Adrien Rabiot doubled Milan's lead in the 43rd minute (assist: low pass from Diego Moreira) with a low strike into the bottom corner; his fourth Serie A goal of the season.
  • Ramos missed a second penalty in the 56th minute (Thomas Kristensen handball blocking Rabiot's shot) — his effort was saved by Atalanta goalkeeper Marco Carnesecchi.
  • Mario Gila added Milan's third in the 57th minute — a close-range header at the far post from Rabiot's flick-on; his first Serie A goal since 2024.
  • Atalanta's goals: Gianluca Gaetano in the 66th minute (assist Nikola Krstovic, tight-angle finish) and Nikola Krstovic in the 81st minute (assist: Raoul Bellanova's cross).
  • Other moments: Christian Pulisic's 13th-minute goal ruled out for offside (VAR); Krstovic hit the post in the 63rd minute; Milan keeper Mike Maignan saved a one-on-one chance from Davide Zappacosta late on; Sead Kolasinac replaced by Obric in the 20th minute; yellow cards for Rabiot (41'), Ederson (68'), and Obric (78').
  • Context: Milan are unbeaten in seven matches — their best start to a season since the 2021-2022 title-winning campaign; first win over Atalanta since 2023; Milan coach: Ruben Amorim.

First Half: Ramos Penalty and Rabiot Strike

AC Milan defeated Atalanta 3-2 in a Serie A matchday 7 clash at San Siro in the early hours of Monday (19/10/2026) WIB. Goals from Goncalo Ramos, Adrien Rabiot, and Mario Gila put the Rossoneri three goals ahead before Atalanta's second-half fightback turned the game into a tense finish.

Milan thought they had broken the deadlock in the 13th minute through Christian Pulisic, but the goal was ruled out for offside after a VAR review. The hosts' lead finally came in the 25th minute: the referee awarded a penalty after VAR confirmed substitute Relja Obric's handball blocking Yunus Musah's shot. Ramos stepped up as the taker and made no mistake — 1-0 to Milan.

Two minutes before the break, the hosts doubled their advantage. Diego Moreira sent a low pass into the penalty area, and Adrien Rabiot met it with a low strike into the bottom corner in the 43rd minute. It was Rabiot's fourth Serie A goal of the season. The 2-0 lead held until half-time.

Ramos Misses Second Penalty, Gila Scores First

Milan were awarded a second penalty in the second half after Thomas Kristensen was judged to have handled Rabiot's shot. However, Ramos' 56th-minute effort was saved by Atalanta goalkeeper Marco Carnesecchi — a miss that nearly came back to haunt them.

A minute later, Milan still managed to extend their lead from a corner. Rabiot flicked the ball on at the near post, and Mario Gila headed home from close range at the far post in the 57th minute. The Spanish defender's first Serie A goal since 2024 put Milan 3-0 up.

Atalanta Fight Back, Maignan to the Rescue

Atalanta had already threatened before Milan's third goal: Nikola Krstovic hit the post in the 63rd minute. Three minutes later, Gianluca Gaetano pulled one back with a tight-angle finish from Krstovic's assist — 3-1 in the 66th minute.

The visitors' pressure produced a second goal in the 81st minute. Raoul Bellanova delivered a low cross from the right, and Krstovic swept it in with an accurate finish to make it 3-2. In the remaining time, Milan goalkeeper Mike Maignan proved the hero with a one-on-one save from Davide Zappacosta, ensuring the three points stayed with the hosts.

Best Start Since the Title-Winning Season

The victory extended Milan's unbeaten run to seven matches — the Rossoneri's best start to a season since the 2021-2022 title-winning campaign. It was also Milan's first win over Atalanta since 2023.

For Milan coach Ruben Amorim, the result keeps his squad's positive momentum going. Mario Gila's first Serie A goal since 2024 and Adrien Rabiot's consistency — now with four goals this season — are valuable assets for Milan in this season's title race.
