Singapore Airlines SQ31 Turns Back to San Francisco

Stateline MEDIA Newsroom•October 17, 2026•2 min read

Singapore Airlines flight SQ31 from San Francisco to Singapore turned back after nearly nine hours in the air after a passenger became unruly and injured themselves. The Airbus A350-900 landed safely; the flight continued later that night with a fresh crew.

Key Facts

  • Singapore Airlines flight SQ31 on the San Francisco-Singapore route turned back to its origin airport on October 14, 2026, after a passenger behaved uncontrollably and injured themselves in the cabin.
  • The Airbus A350-900 departed San Francisco at 11:51 AM local time and landed back around 8:41 PM, after about 8 hours and 40 minutes of flight reaching the North Pacific region.
  • The captain decided to return after the crew assessed the cabin situation; the passenger was handed to local authorities after the aircraft landed safely with no further incidents.
  • The airline did not disclose details of the passenger's actions or injuries; Singapore Airlines said it is cooperating with authorities in handling the matter.
  • The flight continued with a fresh crew, departing San Francisco again around 11:18 PM local time; the airline rearranged passengers' onward schedules.
  • The San Francisco-Singapore route is a 16-17 hour nonstop flight, one of the world's longest routes.

Eight Hours of Flying, Back to the Start

Singapore Airlines flight SQ31 was forced to return to its departure point in San Francisco, United States, after being airborne for nearly nine hours. The turnaround decision was made due to an unruly passenger in the cabin.

The Airbus A350-900 had departed San Francisco for Singapore on Wednesday (10/14) at 11:51 AM local time. According to tracking data cited by The Business Times, the aircraft had flown far into the North Pacific region.

Flight SQ31 finally landed back at San Francisco International Airport around 8:41 PM local time. The San Francisco-Singapore route is one of the world's longest, with an estimated flight time of 16-17 hours.

Captain's Decision for Safety

A Singapore Airlines spokesperson said the passenger was reported to have injured themselves during the flight. The cockpit crew assessed the situation before deciding to turn back.

"A Singapore Airlines passenger on flight SQ31 became unruly and injured themselves in the process," the spokesperson said. The airline did not disclose details of the passenger's actions or the nature of the injuries.

Upon landing, authorities immediately took the unruly passenger into custody for further examination. The airline confirmed the aircraft landed without further incident and all other passengers were safe.

Flight Resumed That Night

After the aircraft returned and the situation was declared resolved, the flight continued with a fresh crew. According to Singapore Airlines, the onward departure to Singapore took place around 11:18 PM local time.

The airline confirmed it provided necessary assistance to passengers, including rearranging onward flight schedules. Singapore Airlines also said it is cooperating with authorities in handling the matter.

Long-haul turnarounds like this are rare and costly, given wasted fuel and crew schedules that must be rebuilt. The captain's decision to return was judged the right call for passenger safety.
