MotoGP 2026: Martin vs. Marquez just two points apart

Stateline MEDIA Newsroom•October 15, 2026•2 min read

The MotoGP 2026 World Championship competition reached its hottest point after 17 rounds: Jorge Martin (Aprilia Racing) led the ranking with 355 points, only two points ahead of Marc Marquez (Ducati Lenovo Team).

Key Facts

  • Jorge Martin (Aprilia Racing) leads the interim MotoGP 2026 ranking with 355 points after 17 rounds, outperforming Marc Marquez (Ducati Lenovo Team, 353 points).
  • The two-point gap equals the smallest record since the previous 17 rounds in 2022, when Fabio Quartararo faced Francesco Bagnaia; then Bagnaia finally became world champion.
  • Marc Marquez cut the deficit from 102 points to two points.
  • At the main race of MotoGP Mandalika, Sunday (11/10/2026), Marco Bezzecchi came out as the champion, Jorge Martin finished third, and Marc Marquez finished sixth.
  • Marco Bezzecchi (309 points) still has a chance in winning the MotoGP 2026 world championship title.
  • The next series takes place on the Phillip Island Circuit, Australia; Marquez insisted his team will focus on their own work facing two Aprilia riders.

Martin vs. Marquez at the top of MotoGP 2026

The MotoGP 2026 World Championship competition entered the determining phase after 17 rounds. Jorge Martin of Aprilia Racing led the interim rankings with 355 points, only two points ahead of Marc Marquez of Ducati Lenovo Team who collected 353 points.

The two-point gap equals the smallest record since the previous 17 rounds in the 2022 season, when Fabio Quartararo faced Francesco Bagnaia. Interestingly, then-backed driver, Bagnaia, finally came out as world champion at the end of the season. This fact adds to the dramatic duel between Martin and Marquez this season.

Marquez Slashes 102-Point Deficit Down to Two

The journey of Marquez pursued Martin into a big story this season.The driver of the Ducati Lenovo Team managed to cut a deficit of 102 points until now there are only two points left.

At the main race of MotoGP Mandalika in the Mandalika Circuit on Sunday (11/10/2026), Martin finished third while Marquez finished sixth.

Bezzecchi Wins at Mandalika, Title Race Still Wide Open

The main MotoGP race of Mandalika was won by Marco Bezzecchi. The victory brought his score to 309, so he still had a chance in winning the 2026 MotoGP World Championship title despite the distance linked quite far from the top two names.

Thus, the interim rankings after the Mandalika place Jorge Martin in the first place with 355 points, followed by Marc Marquez with 353 points, and Marco Bezzecchi with 309 points.

Final Five Rounds: Phillip Island Test Awaits

The next series will take place on the Phillip Island Circuit, Australia. Facing two consistent Aprilia riders, Marquez insisted that his team will focus on their own work and not be affected by the opponent’s strategy.

With five series remaining and only two points of difference, the MotoGP 2026 World Championship title is potentially determined until the final race of this season.

Marquez also refused to rule out Bezzecchi, noting that the Italian rider has already proven fast on every circuit this season and remains firmly in title contention.
