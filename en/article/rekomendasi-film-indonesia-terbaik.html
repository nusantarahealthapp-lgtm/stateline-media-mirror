Best Indonesian Movies of All Time: Top Recommendations

Stateline MEDIA Newsroom•October 14, 2026•2 min read

From The Raid to Laskar Pelangi and Agak Laen, here is a list of the highest-rated Indonesian films that deserve a spot on your watchlist.

Key Facts

  • The Raid (2012) earned a 7.6 rating on IMDb and is known as far as the United States as a world-class action film.
  • Laskar Pelangi (2008) earned 7.9 on IMDb; an adaptation of Andrea Hirata's novel about the struggle for education in Belitung.
  • Cahaya dari Timur: Beta Maluku earned 7.9 on IMDb and won Best Picture at the 2014 Indonesian Film Festival (FFI).
  • Petualangan Sherina (2000) earned 7.8 on IMDb and became Indonesia's legendary adventure musical.
  • Ngeri-Ngeri Sedap (2022) earned 8.0 on IMDb with its touching family comedy-drama.
  • Agak Laen (2024) became the highest-grossing Indonesian film of all time with over 10 million viewers.

World-Class Action: The Raid

Gareth Evans' The Raid (2012) is proof that Indonesian films can compete globally. Following a police special-forces raid on a criminal stronghold inside an apartment block, the film earned a 7.6 on IMDb and praise from international critics.

Its brutal silat choreography and intense cinematography made The Raid famous as far as the United States. If you like no-compromise action, this is your must-watch first.

Inspiring Dramas and True Stories

Laskar Pelangi (2008), adapted from Andrea Hirata's novel, follows the struggles of ten students and their teachers in Belitung. Its 7.9 IMDb rating and status as one of Indonesia's top-grossing films make it a timeless family watch.

Cahaya dari Timur: Beta Maluku (2014) also earned 7.9 on IMDb and won Best Picture at FFI 2014. The story of a children's football coach amid the Ambon conflict was filmed on location and touches on unity.

The Biggest Comedy Hits

Agak Laen (2024) officially became the highest-grossing Indonesian film of all time with over 10 million viewers, breaking previous records. The horror-comedy blend from the podcast quartet proves the power of stories close to audiences.

Don't miss Ngeri-Ngeri Sedap (2022) with its 8.0 IMDb rating — a Batak family comedy-drama that's hilarious and heartwarming — plus the legendary Cek Toko Sebelah (2016).

Timeless Classics

Ada Apa Dengan Cinta? (2002) with its 7.7 IMDb rating redefined Indonesian teen films and created the Rangga-Cinta pop culture icons. Petualangan Sherina (2000) with 7.8 is a legendary adventure musical captivating across generations.

For adventurous souls, 5 cm (2012) with a 7.2 rating takes five friends climbing Mahameru in a heartwarming journey of friendship.

Where to Watch These Films

Most films on this list are available on legal streaming platforms. The Raid and its sequel can be watched on Netflix, while dramas like Laskar Pelangi and Ngeri-Ngeri Sedap frequently appear on Netflix, Disney+ Hotstar, Vidio, and Prime Video.

Title availability may change with licensing periods, so check your favorite streaming app directly. Watching through official channels is also a way to support Indonesian filmmakers so they keep creating.
