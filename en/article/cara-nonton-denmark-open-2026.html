Cara Nonton Denmark Open 2026: Live Streaming Vidio

The 2026 Denmark Open streams live exclusively on Vidio. The BWF World Tour Super 750 event runs October 13-18, 2026, at the Jyske Bank Arena in Odense, Denmark, featuring 14 Indonesian shuttlers.

Key Facts

  • The 2026 Denmark Open — a BWF World Tour Super 750 event — runs October 13-18, 2026, at the Jyske Bank Arena in Odense, Denmark.
  • Live streaming airs exclusively on the Vidio OTT platform; viewers need an active subscription package to watch.
  • Matches start at 14:00 WIB (Western Indonesian Time) daily, with Indonesian players spread across three courts.
  • Alternatives: BWF TV on YouTube and real-time live scores on the official BWF website; the official broadcast list is on BWF's “Where to Watch” page.
  • Total prize money is USD 950,000; Indonesia sent 14 representatives across five disciplines.
  • Schedules and broadcast platforms may change at the rights holder's discretion.

Vidio Is the Official Live Streaming Platform

The easiest way to watch the 2026 Denmark Open is via Vidio. The Victor Denmark Open 2026 badminton tournament streams live exclusively on the Vidio OTT service.

Indonesian badminton fans need to activate a subscription package first. Besides the live broadcast, every court's matches can also be followed in real time through the live score feature on the official BWF website.

As a Super 750 event, the Denmark Open always draws the world's top players and offers crucial ranking points.

Matches Start at 14:00 WIB

Denmark Open 2026 matches begin at 14:00 WIB (Western Indonesian Time) each day across three courts at the Jyske Bank Arena in Odense. On day one, Tuesday (13/10/2026), six Indonesians played first.

On day two, Wednesday (14/10/2026), eight Indonesians take the court, including Jonatan Christie vs Taiwan's Lin Chun-yi at 17:30 WIB on Court 2.

The early rounds through the finals run October 13-18, 2026. Check the daily order of play, as match order can shift depending on earlier match durations.

Alternatives: BWF TV and Live Score

Beyond Vidio, fans can also follow the 2026 Denmark Open via BWF TV on YouTube. The BWF published its official broadcast list, “Where to Watch: VICTOR Denmark Open 2026,” on October 14, 2026.

The BWF advises viewers to check their local TV guides for broadcast times and details in their region.

For those who cannot watch video, the live score feature on the official BWF World Tour website shows real-time scoring from every court.

14 Indonesian Representatives in Odense

Indonesia sent 14 representatives across five disciplines: men's singles, women's singles, men's doubles, women's doubles, and mixed doubles. Men's singles features Jonatan Christie, Alwi Farhan, and Moh Zaki Ubaidillah.

Women's singles is represented by Putri Kusuma Wardani, while men's doubles fields five pairs, including Fajar Alfian/Muhammad Shohibul Fikri.

USD 950,000 in prize money is at stake, along with 750 ranking points. Six Indonesians were already eliminated on day one, so hopes now rest on the eight playing on day two.
