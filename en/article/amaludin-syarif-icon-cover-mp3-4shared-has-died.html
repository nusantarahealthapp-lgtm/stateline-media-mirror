News on social media

JAKARTA — The sad news comes from Amaludin Syarif, a figure whose photo is widely known as an MP3 file cover in the era of 4shared and Stafaband downloads.

The news came after the Threads @cryandthemum account uploaded a message that was later discussed by many citizens.

Iconic Face of MP3

For the 1990s to the early 2000s, Amaludin Syarif's face was a familiar landscape. His photographs often appeared as a cover or an album of MP3 art files downloaded from 4shared and Stafaband file sharing sites, although he was not a singer or public figure.

The origin of the photo was simple. Amaludin collected his favourite Indonesian songs and uploaded them to 4shared, and installed his own photo as an album cover of the files. His MP3 files were downloaded and distributed by other users, until his face spread widely into a familiar visual marker on the Indonesian internet.

“I am not an artist.”

Amaludin was embarrassed when he found out that his photo was famous as an MP3 cover.He called himself not a public figure, with a simple sentence: "I am not an artist."His day-to-day life continued beyond his unplanned fame — he worked as an agent in one of the Telkom Group's subsidiaries.

His story returned viral in January 2026 after he was invited to the television show "Ambyar Morning" to tell the beginning of his photo spread. On social media, he was nicknamed "Mas Stafaband", a loving nickname from citizens to men behind the legendary face of the MP3 download era.

The Icon of Nostalgia

The news of his departure sparked a wave of nostalgia and rhetoric on social media. Many citizens remembered how his face accompanied their youth when downloading songs for songs to be listened through Winamp and mobile music players.

Until this news is written, there is no further official statement from the family about the cause of Amaludin Syarif’s death.Good day, Mas Stafaband — your face will always be attached to the Indonesian internet memory.
