'Breaking Bad' Star RJ Mitte Dies at 34

Sad news from Hollywood. RJ Mitte (Roy Frank Mitte III), the actor known for playing Walter "Flynn" White Jr. in the phenomenon series Breaking Bad, died on Monday (10/12) in Brownsville, Texas, United States. He was 34.

Mitte's death was confirmed by his representatives in a written statement to the media. "It is with profound sadness we share the tragic passing of beloved actor, philanthropist, and passionate disability rights advocate RJ Mitte," the statement read, as quoted by several outlets.

Brownsville police said they received a call around 12:50 p.m. local time before officers rushed to Mitte's residence. Police stressed the official determination of cause and manner of death still awaits the investigation's results.

Representatives Confirm Cause of Death

Mitte's representative, Sean Reilly, confirmed to multiple outlets that the actor died by suicide. TMZ, which first broke the story, said a county official confirmed Mitte's death to them, with the law enforcement investigation still ongoing.

Note: Stateline MEDIA offers its deepest condolences and chooses not to publish unnecessary details. If you or someone you know is struggling or in crisis, call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or contact your local emergency services.

Weeks before his passing, Mitte had been photographed with Breaking Bad co-star Luis Moncada, and both appeared in good spirits, according to social media posts.

Bryan Cranston's Emotional Tribute

Mitte's passing sparked an outpouring of grief from fans and fellow cast members. Bryan Cranston, who played Walter White — the father of Mitte's character in the series — wrote an emotional tribute on Instagram.

Cranston wrote that he met Mitte when the actor was only 14, when he was cast as Walter White Jr. — a part he played with such joy and enthusiasm. He called the loss an extraordinary grief, especially since they had been texting each other and planning lunch together just weeks earlier.

"I'll miss you, son," Cranston wrote, addressing Mitte as the father-son bond they shared on screen. Breaking Bad fans flooded social media with condolences and memories of the Walter White Jr. character.

Career Journey and Activism Legacy

Born August 21, 1992, in Jackson, Mississippi, Roy Frank Mitte III was diagnosed with cerebral palsy at age three. His character Walter White Jr. shared the same condition, and Mitte brought his own lived experience to give authenticity to the role across 56 episodes of the AMC series that ran from 2008 to 2013 — often called one of the greatest TV series of all time.

Before that breakthrough, Mitte appeared in minor roles in series such as Hannah Montana and Everybody Hates Chris. His career continued into films like The Oak Room, The Recall, The Guardians of Justice, and Triumph, which he also executive produced.

Off screen, Mitte was a vocal activist. He served as an ambassador for United Cerebral Palsy and Shriners Children's, president of The Mitte Foundation, and a Screen Actors Guild spokesman for actors with disabilities. He used his platform to challenge stereotypes and fight for better representation of people with disabilities.
